- Pilot. Start cold on L1 SA-neg, at most six OpenMP threads, 300 steady iterations and 10 minutes. Keep the native log, restart, mesh, wall CSV and flow field. Stop this session on process error, nonfinite state, nonpositive density or pressure, or a wall-clock cap. Use same-iteration inbound and outbound quantities and document the extraction. Report both sign-change locations of bottom-wall Cf in x = 0.30–0.45 m, the Cp level and near-wall y+. Absent a crossing on the same support, report that outcome. Residual decrease is diagnostic. A 1 mm floor is a reporting tolerance for this geometry and finite sampling.

Do not start Mach 2.05 until the first native path delivers credible, recoverable outputs and the current major uncertainty has been addressed.
- Operate from this isolated directory in the current monitored chat. Check process and log growth during runs, and send a status update on important failure, hold or completion here.
