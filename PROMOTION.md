# MachAtlas launch copy

## English

**MachAtlas — a supersonic flow atlas.** See the physics behind supersonic flight.

Explore three real wind-tunnel cases, from a Mach 2.05 ceiling ramp to two Mach 3 experiments. Move a 3 mm radial band and see a model comparison reverse while the flow measurements stay the same.

[Explore the atlas](https://hengyuhang61-creator.github.io/machatlas/) · [v0.1.0](https://github.com/hengyuhang61-creator/machatlas/releases/tag/v0.1.0)

**MachAtlas — 高速流动物理图鉴。** 看见超声速飞行背后的物理。

## Technical community draft

How far from the wall should you judge a turbulence model? In one published Mach 2.5 comparison, moving the same 3 mm window changed which model had lower velocity bias. MachAtlas lets you try it on the archived data and replay all three cases from pinned inputs. [Interactive case](https://hengyuhang61-creator.github.io/machatlas/cone.html) · [Sources](https://github.com/hengyuhang61-creator/machatlas/blob/v0.1.0/SOURCES.md)
